System design

Three tiers, one record

Sensor edge
ESP32 · 2× INMP441 VAG mics · MPU6050 on shank · HX711 load cell. Framing, band RMS and MFCC at 100 ms hops on-chip; only features go over BLE.
Phone edge
Android app, TFLite GBT model (184 KB), SQLite WAL store, per-patient encryption. Full screening works with the radio off.
PHC / district
Delta sync on reconnect, conflict-free by device+timestamp keys. Supervisor dashboard, referral tracking, model retraining set.
